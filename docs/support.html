Daywell support

Help with Screen Time access, selected apps, daily blocks, and your shared allowance.

Getting started

  1. Install Daywell on an iPhone running iOS 17.4 or later.
  2. Tap Enable Screen Time access and approve Apple's permission sheet.
  3. Tap Choose apps & start. In Apple's picker, expand a category and select individual apps. Daywell does not currently support selecting whole categories or websites.
  4. Review the daily blocks and shared allowance, then tap Activate protection.

Why is an app still available?

Check that Screen Time access is still enabled and that the app appears under Blocked apps in Daywell. A scheduled window must be active or the shared allowance must be used up. iOS controls Screen Time callbacks and may apply changes after a delay. Open Daywell to see any monitoring error.

How do edits take effect?

Adding selected apps and lowering the allowance can apply immediately between scheduled blocks. Removing or replacing apps and increasing the allowance take effect the next day, after an active overnight block ends. During a scheduled block, Daywell locks the rule editors until that block ends.

Can I turn protection off?

Daywell's shield has a Close button without an in-app skip option. The device owner can revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings or uninstall Daywell. Daywell is a self-management tool, not device management software.
