Daywell

A little more room in your day.

Choose the apps you want to block, set daily windows and a shared allowance, and let iOS help keep your boundaries in place.

Your routine, your choice

Daywell uses Apple's private Screen Time picker. You select individual apps and choose when to block them. Time spent across those apps counts toward one shared daily allowance.

Simple by design

Daywell works without an account, subscription, ads, analytics, or a server. It requires iOS 17.4 or later and your Screen Time permission.

Help and privacy

Get support or read the privacy policy.

Blocking depends on iOS Screen Time callbacks and can be delayed. You can revoke permission or uninstall the app at any time.
